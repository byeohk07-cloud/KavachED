# Changelog

All notable changes to KavachED are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added
- Streamlit student support app with teacher and student workspaces.
- PBKDF2 account authentication and temporary failed-login lockout.
- Demo college data, explainable risk scoring, and attendance planning.
- Workspace document retrieval with optional Groq answer generation.
- Teacher roster, record entry, interventions, and student support views.
- Student overview, subject detail, study guidance, and what-if planning.
- Theme styling, logos, tests, and project documentation.
